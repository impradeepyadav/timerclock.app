How to Use the Online Countdown Timer

Need to keep track of time? Timer Clock makes it simple. Choose a preset time (1, 5, 10, 15, 25, 30, 45, or 60 minutes), or set your own by entering hours, minutes, and seconds. Click Start to begin the countdown. The circular progress ring shows how much time is left as the timer runs.

Once the countdown reaches zero, an alert sound lets you know that the time is up. You can turn the sound on or off using the speaker button. Need a better view? Switch to fullscreen mode to display the timer on a larger screen. It's handy for classrooms, workouts, cooking, meetings, and presentations.

Popular Ways to Use an Online Timer

  • Kitchen and egg timer — Keep track of cooking times, boiling eggs, and baking without constantly checking the clock.
  • Study and Pomodoro timer — Set 25-minute study sessions and take short breaks to stay focused.
  • Workout and interval timer — Track exercise rounds, rest periods, HIIT sessions, and daily workouts.
  • Meditation timer — Set aside 5, 10, or 15 minutes for quiet time, breathing exercises, or meditation.
  • Meeting and presentation timer — Keep track of speaking time and make sure meetings stay on schedule.
  • Exam timer — Set a time limit for practice tests, quizzes, and revision sessions.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Pomodoro timer?

A Pomodoro timer helps you stay focused by breaking work into short sessions, usually 25 minutes, followed by a short break. You can use the 25-minute preset or visit our Pomodoro timer for focused study and work sessions.

Is Timer Clock free to use?

Yes, Timer Clock is free to use. There's no account to create or software to install. Just open the website and set your timer whenever you need it.

Can I use the countdown timer offline?

Yes, the timer runs directly in your browser without needing an internet connection once the page has loaded. You only need to go online again if you want to reload the page.

Can I change the language on Timer Clock?

Yes, you can change the website language using the language selector in the top-right corner. On mobile devices, you'll find it inside the menu. Available languages include English, Hindi, Spanish, French, and German.

What is Timer Clock and who runs it?

Timer Clock is a free online tool for setting countdown timers and managing everyday tasks. To learn more about the website, its purpose, and how to get in touch, visit our About page.

Can I use the timer in fullscreen mode?

Yes, you can switch to fullscreen mode to make the countdown easier to see from a distance. It's useful when cooking, exercising, teaching, or giving a presentation.
