How to use the online alarm clock

Timer Clock makes it easy to set an alarm right from your browser. Choose the hour and minute, select AM or PM if you're using the 12-hour format, and add a label such as "Wake up" or "Meeting" to remember what the alarm is for. When you're ready, click Add Alarm. At the scheduled time, you'll hear a sound alert that continues until you press STOP ALARM.

The clock automatically selects a 12-hour or 24-hour format based on your browser's language and regional settings. Prefer a different format? Simply use the 12H / 24H toggle to switch whenever you like. Your preference is saved for your next visit. In 24-hour mode, you can also see your local timezone next to the clock.

Your alarms are saved in your browser, so they remain available when you reload the page on the same device and browser. You can turn individual alarms on or off, delete the ones you no longer need, and switch to fullscreen mode for a clearer view of the clock.

What can you use an online alarm clock for?

An online alarm clock is useful for more than just waking up. You can use it throughout the day whenever you need a reminder or want to stay on schedule.

  • Waking up: Set a morning alarm on your computer or tablet.
  • Daily reminders: Remember meetings, appointments, and important tasks.
  • Cooking: Keep track of baking, food preparation, and other kitchen tasks.
  • Power naps: Set a short alarm so you don't sleep longer than planned.
  • Study sessions: Set reminders for study breaks and revision time.
  • Workouts: Keep track of exercise intervals and scheduled activities.

Frequently asked questions

How do I set an alarm online?

Select the hour and minute, then choose AM or PM if you're using the 12-hour format. You can also add a label to identify your alarm. Click Add Alarm to save it. The alarm will play a sound when the selected time arrives.

Can I switch between 12-hour and 24-hour time?

Yes. You can switch between 12-hour and 24-hour formats using the 12H / 24H toggle. The clock also detects your preferred format from your browser's regional settings, and your selection is saved.

Does the online alarm clock use my local timezone?

Yes. The clock uses your device's local time to schedule alarms. In 24-hour mode, your timezone is also displayed next to the clock.

Are my alarms saved automatically?

Yes. Your alarms are stored in your browser's localStorage. They remain saved after you reload the page, as long as you continue using the same browser and device without clearing its stored data.

Is this online alarm clock free to use?

Yes. Timer Clock's online alarm clock is free to use. You don't need to create an account or install any software. Just open the page and set your alarm.

Will my alarm ring if I close the browser tab?

No. The alarm needs the webpage to remain open in your browser. Keep the tab open and your device powered on to receive the alarm. Background behavior may vary depending on your browser and device settings.

Can I set more than one alarm?

Yes. You can create multiple alarms, each with its own time and optional label. You can also turn individual alarms on or off or delete them whenever you want.
