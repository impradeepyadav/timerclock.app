How to use the 25 minute timer

This page is a free 25 minute timer already preset to 25:00. Press Start (or hit the Space bar) and the countdown begins instantly. When time reaches zero, a sound alert plays — perfect for marking the end of a focus block.

Need a different length? Use the presets at the top (1, 5, 10, 15, 25, 30, 45 or 60 minutes) or type any custom time in the hour / minute / second inputs. Enter fullscreen for a distraction-free display — great for classrooms, gyms, or a bedside clock.

Why 25 minutes?

Twenty-five minutes is the classic length of one Pomodoro — the standard focus block in the Pomodoro Technique created by Francesco Cirillo. It's long enough to make real progress on a single task, and short enough that starting doesn't feel intimidating. Four Pomodoros (with short 5-minute breaks between them) fill roughly two hours of productive work.

Frequently asked questions

How do I start the 25 minute timer?

The timer is already set to 25:00 when the page loads. Press Start (or the Space bar) and the countdown begins immediately. A sound plays when it reaches zero.

Why is 25 minutes the standard Pomodoro length?

25 minutes is the length used in the Pomodoro Technique by Francesco Cirillo: long enough to make real progress on a task, short enough that starting feels manageable. Four 25-minute focus blocks plus short breaks fill roughly two focused hours.

Can I change the time?

Yes. Use the presets (1, 5, 10, 15, 25, 30, 45, 60 minutes) or type a custom time in the hour / minute / second inputs. The 25-minute default is just a starting point.

Is the 25 minute timer free?

Yes. It's completely free, requires no signup, and runs directly in your browser on any device (phone, tablet, or computer).

Does it work in the background?

Yes, once the page loads the countdown runs locally in your browser. For the sound alert to play reliably, keep the tab visible or the device screen on — the timer uses the Wake Lock API to keep the screen awake.
