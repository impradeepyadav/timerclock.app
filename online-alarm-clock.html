How to use the online alarm clock

Timer Clock's online alarm clock shows the live current time and lets you set one or more alarms. Enter the hour, minute, and AM/PM, optionally add a label like "Wake up" or "Meeting", then click Add Alarm. When the clock reaches that time, an alarm sounds repeatedly until you press STOP ALARM.

All your alarms are saved automatically in your browser, so they persist across page reloads on the same device and browser. Toggle any alarm on or off with the switch, or remove it with the delete button. Enter fullscreen for a distraction-free clock display.

Frequently asked questions

How do I set an alarm?

Enter the hour, minute, and AM/PM, optionally add a label, then click Add Alarm. The alarm will ring with a sound at the chosen time.

Are my alarms saved?

Yes. All alarms are saved in your browser's localStorage, so they persist across page reloads on the same device and browser.

Is the online alarm clock free?

Yes. Timer Clock's alarm clock is completely free, requires no signup, and runs directly in your browser.

Will it wake me up if the tab is closed?

No. The alarm only works while the page is open in a browser tab. Keep the tab open and your device on for the alarm to ring.

Can I set multiple alarms?

Yes. You can add as many alarms as you like, each with its own time and optional label. Toggle them on or off individually.
