How to use the online stopwatch

Timer Clock's online stopwatch measures elapsed time with millisecond precision. Press Start to begin counting up. While running, press Lap to record a split without stopping. Press Pause to freeze the count, then Resume to continue, or Reset to clear everything and start fresh.

Use the Space key to start or pause, L to record a lap, and R to reset. Enter fullscreen for a distraction-free display — great for classrooms, gyms, or desktop use.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use the online stopwatch?

Press Start to begin counting. Press Lap to record a lap time while running, Pause to stop temporarily, and Reset to clear and start over.

Does the stopwatch show milliseconds?

Yes. Elapsed time is displayed in MM:SS.mmm format with millisecond precision, switching to HH:MM:SS.mmm after one hour.

Is the online stopwatch free?

Yes. Our stopwatch is completely free, requires no signup, and runs directly in your browser on any device.

What do the green and red lap colors mean?

The fastest lap is highlighted in green and the slowest in red, so you can instantly spot your best and worst splits.
