How to Use the Online Stopwatch

Need to keep track of time? Timer Clock's online stopwatch makes it easy. Click Start to begin counting, then use Lap whenever you want to record a split without stopping the timer. You can pause at any point and pick up where you left off with Resume. If you want to start over, just hit Reset.

You can also control the stopwatch with your keyboard. Press Space to start or pause, L to record a lap, and R to reset. Need a bigger display? Switch to fullscreen to keep the time visible while you work out, teach a class, or run an activity.

What Can You Use a Stopwatch For?

  • Workouts and training – Keep track of exercise rounds, rest periods, and workout times.
  • Cooking and baking – Time each step of a recipe without losing track.
  • Running and cycling – Record lap times and compare your performance.
  • Studying and work – See how long you spend on a task or study session.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the online stopwatch?

Click Start to begin counting. Use Lap to save a split time without stopping the stopwatch. Click Pause when you need a break, Resume to continue, or Reset to clear the time and start again.

Does the stopwatch show milliseconds?

Yes. The stopwatch displays time down to milliseconds in MM:SS.mmm format. Once an hour has passed, the display changes to HH:MM:SS.mmm.

Is the online stopwatch free to use?

Yes, it's completely free. There's no signup or installation needed. Just open Timer Clock in your browser and start using the stopwatch.

What do the green and red lap times mean?

Green marks your fastest lap, while red highlights your slowest. This makes it easier to compare your splits and see where you were quickest or slowest.
